Trust & Security

Your vault stays with you.

GetMine is built so your health information can work for you without giving up control. This is how we approach trust and security today, and what we’re building toward.

Local-first by design

Your vault lives on your device. The day-to-day work of organising and recalling your information happens locally, on hardware you own.

Minimum necessary exposure

We aim to send only the smallest slice of context needed for each task, and only when you ask. Your full health record never has to leave your vault.

You decide what leaves

Sharing happens by choice. You decide what is shared, with whom, and for how long. You can revoke access at any time.

Transparency matters

We want you to be able to see how GetMine works, what it does with your information, and what is changing. We’ll continue to publish plain-English documentation as the product matures.

A foundation, not a destination

Security is foundational and we treat it that way. As GetMine matures, our security posture will continue to deepen — more controls, more transparency, and progressively more of the architecture running at the edge.

Honest about today

We’re a small team in closed beta. We have not yet sought formal certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001) and we don’t make claims we can’t back up. We will pursue the right ones as the product and the team grow. Until then, what we promise is what we build: a local-first vault, minimum necessary exposure, and your data under your control.

Questions

Write to privacy@getmine.ai for any trust or security question. We’ll answer honestly.
