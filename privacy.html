Privacy

Your health information belongs to you.

This is a short, plain-English summary of how we handle your information at GetMine. A fuller privacy notice will accompany the public launch — until then, this is what to expect.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the service and improve it: the information you choose to add or connect to your vault, contact details for your account, and the product feedback you give us during Early Access. Where information has to pass through the cloud (for example, to answer an AI query), it does so only as needed to complete the task.

We don’t sell your data

GetMine does not sell personal information. We don’t trade access to your vault for partnerships or revenue. We grow by deepening what stays with you — not by leveraging your data.

You stay in control

You decide what is shared, with whom, and for how long. You can change your mind, and you can ask us to delete information you’ve added. Our role is to organise and help — not to keep something you no longer want kept.

During Early Access

As a closed-beta participant you may share contact details, give us product feedback, and choose what to add or connect to your vault. We treat all of that as your information, on your terms.

Access, deletion, questions

Write to privacy@getmine.ai for any privacy question — including requests to access or delete the information we hold about you.
